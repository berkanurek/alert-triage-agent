# PB-05 Data exfiltration and insider risk

## When to use
Large uploads to personal cloud storage, personal email or USB devices, especially from shares with confidential client data.

## Investigation
Check the user's employment status: leaving employees and users moving to competitors are the highest risk. Check the data classification of the source share and whether any business approval exists.

## Response
Severity is high when confidential data goes to a personal account without approval, critical if the user is leaving for a competitor and client data is involved. Block the destination, preserve logs, and involve HR, Legal and Compliance before contacting the user. Do not alert the user first.
